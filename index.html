Research

Arithmetic questions, special elements, and Iwasawa theory.

My research concerns arithmetic structures arising from Galois representations, special elements, and Iwasawa theory, with connections to refined conjectures for L-functions.

Theme 01

Iwasawa theory

Questions concerning main conjectures, higher-rank phenomena, and arithmetic over global fields.

Theme 02

Special elements

Higher special elements of p-adic representations and the information they encode in Galois cohomology.

Theme 03

Arithmetic geometry

Elliptic curves, abelian varieties, and Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer type questions in positive characteristic.
